Organic Agriculture Production NCII (32 days)

Agricultural Crops Production NCII (45 days)


![Class](/class.jpg)


## Schedule of Classes:

Monday – Saturday 8:00AM – 5:00PM

Qualifications of Beneficiaries:

1. Filipino Citizen
2. At least 18 years of age at the time that the individual would finish the training program
3. Enrolled in the National Coconut Farmers Registry System (NCFRS) or with signed enrollment form for the NCRFS and
4. Other Trainee Entry Requirements specific to the qualifications with Training Regulations

## Entry Trainee Requirements:

a. NSO or PSA Birth Certificate and / or Government Issued Valid ID
b. Barangay Clearance
c. Form 137 or Diploma
d. ID Picture 2pcs (1x1) white background
e. Passport Size ID Picture with Name (First Name, MI, Surname) with collar, white background and must be chemically printed

## Scholarship Benefits:

1. Free Skills Training
2. Free Assessment and Certification, if applicable
3. Training Support Fund / Training Allowance (160/day)
4. New Normal Assistance (P1000/scholar)
5. Accident insurance coverage for a period of 1 year
