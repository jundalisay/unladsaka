The Department of Agriculture (DA) has officially launched its much-anticipated ₱2 billion support package aimed at boosting rice production across the Philippines for the 2025 planting season.

## Key Program Components

The package includes three major components designed to address the most pressing needs of rice farmers nationwide:

**Subsidized Inputs**: Certified rice seeds will be distributed at ₱20/kg (down from the market rate of ₱45/kg), and urea fertilizers at ₱900 per 50kg bag.

**Farm Machinery Access**: Qualified farmer cooperatives can apply for zero-interest loans to purchase hand tractors, threshers, and dryers through the Agricultural Credit Policy Council.

**Insurance Coverage**: All registered beneficiaries will receive automatic coverage under the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC) for weather disturbances and pest damage.

## How to Apply

Farmers must register at their local Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) before January 31, 2025. Requirements include:
- Proof of land tenure (ownership, lease, or CARP documents)
- Barangay certification as active farmer
- Valid government-issued ID
