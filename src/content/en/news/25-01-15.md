## Expanding Reach Nationwide

Unlad Saka is proud to announce the rollout of our Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) Training Program to 10 regions beginning February 2025. This initiative, developed in partnership with the Bureau of Soils and Water Management (BSWM), equips farmers with practical tools to adapt to increasingly unpredictable weather patterns.

## What Farmers Will Learn

Each three-day workshop covers:

1. **Water-Efficient Irrigation**: Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) technique for rice can reduce water use by 30% while maintaining yield.
2. **Cover Cropping**: Using mungbean and cowpea as cover crops to restore soil nitrogen between main crop seasons.
3. **Weather App Training**: Using PAGASA's Climate Information Services mobile app for planting decisions.

## Schedule & Registration

Workshops run from February to April 2025. Sessions will be held in Ilocos Norte, Pangasinan, Pampanga, Batangas, Camarines Sur, Iloilo, Cebu, Davao del Sur, Bukidnon, and Lanao del Norte.

Registration is free for Unlad Saka members. Non-members may join at a ₱500 materials fee.
