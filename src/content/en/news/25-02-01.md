## A Historic Partnership

Unlad Saka is proud to announce the formal establishment of its first chapters in the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM), following a memorandum of agreement signed with the BARMM Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, and Agrarian Reform (MAFAR) on January 28, 2025.

## What This Means for BARMM Farmers

The 15,000 farmers registered with MAFAR's programs in Maguindanao del Norte, Maguindanao del Sur, and Lanao del Sur will gain automatic associate membership in Unlad Saka, with access to:

- **Training programs** offered in Filipino and Maguindanaon
- **Market linkages** with buyers in Metro Manila and Cebu
- **Crop insurance** facilitation through PCIC
- **Seedling subsidies** for corn, vegetables, and abaca

## Opening Ceremonies

A ceremonial launching will be held on February 15, 2025 in Cotabato City. Farmers who wish to attend may coordinate with their local MAFAR office or contact Unlad Saka's Mindanao regional coordinator.

This expansion brings Unlad Saka's total provincial reach to **75 provinces**, reinforcing our commitment to truly national representation for Filipino farmers.
